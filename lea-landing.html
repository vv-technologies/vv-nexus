What is LEA? Ce este LEA?

A local-first AI workspace built for people working on long-term projects.

Unlike traditional AI chat tools, LEA remembers context between sessions. It organizes your notes, ideas, project folders and unfinished work — so you can continue naturally without rebuilding context every single day.

LEA is built for project continuity, not just conversation. It works locally on your device, with no cloud sync and no accounts required.

  • Organize project chaos — drop notes, ideas or raw text and LEA structures them automatically
  • Persistent AI memory — context carries across sessions, no more cold restarts
  • Break down large goals — tell LEA what you want to build and it creates clear next steps
  • Local-first AI workspace — everything stays on your device, works fully offline
  • Built for unfinished work — designed for people with too many open projects

LEA is not another AI chatbot. It's a contextual AI workspace designed around the way real solo work actually happens.
