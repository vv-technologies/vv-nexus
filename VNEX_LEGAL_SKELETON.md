# VNEX Legal Skeleton

This document is a product and governance draft for the main legal layers VNEX should eventually publish.

It is not final legal advice.
It is a structure draft that should later be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before launch.

## Purpose

VNEX needs a legal base that protects:

- the platform
- the company
- the founder
- creators
- explorers
- user data
- ownership boundaries

The legal structure should be strong enough that the platform does not depend on personal manual judgment alone.

## Core Legal Documents

VNEX should eventually publish at least these core documents:

### 1. Terms Of Use

This document should define:

- who may use the platform
- account responsibilities
- acceptable use rules
- prohibited behavior
- creator responsibilities
- explorer responsibilities
- access and testing rules
- ownership and platform license rules
- suspension and termination rules
- disclaimers
- limitation of liability
- governing law and dispute basics

### 2. Privacy Policy

This document should define:

- what personal data is collected
- what platform activity is logged
- how data is used
- how data is stored
- what third parties are used
- how users can request deletion or access
- how cookies, analytics, and authentication data are handled

### 3. IP And Copyright Policy

This document should define:

- how ownership is treated
- how copyright complaints are handled
- how takedown requests work
- how repeat infringers are treated
- how disputed uploads are reviewed
- how platform cooperation works in abuse cases

### 4. Community And Access Rules

This document should define:

- access behavior expectations
- preview restrictions
- anti-leak rules
- tester behavior
- feedback behavior
- collaboration boundaries
- reporting standards

### 5. Creator Terms Or Commercial Terms

If VNEX later charges creators or processes paid access, it should also define:

- subscription terms
- refund logic
- paid access rules
- creator billing
- payout rules if any revenue-sharing exists
- taxes and invoicing boundaries

## Key Legal Principles

### Ownership Principle

Creators keep ownership of their own works unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

### Limited Platform License Principle

VNEX only receives the rights needed to operate the platform and the creator's selected visibility and access settings.

### No Guarantee Principle

VNEX should not guarantee that all user-submitted content is original, lawful, or error-free.

Instead, VNEX should provide:

- rules
- reporting
- moderation
- takedown process
- enforcement

### Platform Control Principle

VNEX must preserve the right to:

- remove content
- suspend access
- investigate abuse
- enforce platform rules
- respond to legal complaints

### User Risk Principle

Testing, listening, previewing, and early access should be framed with reasonable disclaimers.

Unreleased work may be:

- incomplete
- unstable
- experimental
- inaccurate
- subject to change

## Anti-Abuse Legal Areas

VNEX should explicitly address:

- unauthorized copying
- leak behavior
- harassment
- impersonation
- scam behavior
- access abuse
- account misuse
- fraudulent commercial behavior

## Privacy And Logging Areas

Because VNEX is access-sensitive, its policies should likely cover:

- account identity data
- access request data
- action logs
- moderation logs
- security logs
- payment-related records if added

The policy should be honest about what is logged and why.

## Founder And Company Protection

The legal stack should help ensure that platform operation does not rest personally on the founder's informal judgment.

The public framing should be:

- the platform has rules
- the company operates the platform
- users accept platform terms
- disputes and reports follow process

This is much safer than a personality-based model.

## Launch Rule

Before a serious public VNEX launch, the platform should have at minimum:

- Terms Of Use
- Privacy Policy
- IP And Copyright Policy
- reporting path
- moderation path

## Summary

The VNEX legal skeleton should be built around:

- creator ownership
- limited platform license
- controlled access
- transparent logging
- enforceable rules
- company-level protection

This document is the structure draft, not the final legal text.
