VNEX — A universe for every builder.

VNEX is a platform where indie builders, developers, game creators and makers document and share their projects in public — while keeping full control over what stays private.

🌐
Your Universe
Every builder owns a Universe — a public space to share projects, logs and progress.
🔨
Follow Projects
Not a feed of profiles. A window into what people are building, where they're stuck and what they need.
⚡
Hybrid by default
Keep your work local. Share only what you choose. You decide what is public and what stays private.
🧪
Real feedback
Find testers, get ideas, receive bug reports. Direct connection between builder and community.
🇷🇴 Currently in Romanian Alpha

VNEX is currently being tested with our first Romanian builders. We are validating the core experience before expanding internationally. English support, international builders and multi-language projects are coming soon.
