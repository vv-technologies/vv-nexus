VNEX — A place for builders.

A platform where indie builders, developers and makers document and share their projects in public — while keeping full control over what stays private.

🔨
Follow projects, not people. Progress matters more than popularity.
💬
Real feedback. Find testers, get ideas, receive actual bug reports.
⚡
Hybrid by default. You decide what is public. Everything else stays yours.
💻
Laptop first. Mobile version coming after validation.
Currently testing with our first Romanian builders. International launch coming soon — not a social network, a builder platform.
